# TechCart Wi-Fi 6E Router Manual

SKU: ROUTER-WIFI6E-TC

## Setup

1. Connect the router WAN port to the modem.
2. Power on the router and wait until the status LED turns solid blue.
3. Open the TechCart Connect app and select "Add Router".
4. Follow the app prompts to name the 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz networks.

## Troubleshooting Wi-Fi Drops

If clients disconnect repeatedly, update firmware first. Then separate 2.4 GHz and 6 GHz SSIDs, move IoT devices to 2.4 GHz, and keep the router away from microwaves, metal shelves, and dense concrete walls.

## Compatibility

Wi-Fi 6E works with Wi-Fi 6E capable devices on the 6 GHz band. Older Wi-Fi 5 and Wi-Fi 6 devices can still connect over 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz.

## Warranty

The router includes a 24-month warranty covering manufacturing defects and firmware-related failures under normal use.
