# TechCart TN-200 ANC True Wireless Earbuds — India Quick Guide

SKU: **TN-200ANC-EARBUDS**

## Box contents

- Earbuds (L/R), charging case, USB-C cable (Type-A to Type-C), two extra silicone tip pairs, quick-start card.

## Charging (230 V mains via any 5 V USB adapter)

1. Case charges via USB-C. Use a **5 V / ≥1 A** adapter; avoid laptop USB suspend ports for first full charge.
2. Full case charge from empty: about **90 minutes**. Earbuds in case: about **45 minutes** to 100%.
3. LED: **solid white** = charging; **off** = full.

## Pairing (Android / iOS)

1. Open case, leave buds inside 10 seconds until LEDs breathe.
2. On phone: **Bluetooth → Pair new device → TechCart TN-200**.
3. Grant **phone** permission if prompted (needed for firmware updates in the TechCart app).

## ANC and transparency

- **Long-press right bud 2s** cycles **ANC on → transparency → off**.
- Wind noise on balconies: switch to **transparency** or **off**; ANC can amplify wind.

## Troubleshooting

### Left bud quiet or dead

1. Clean contact pins on bud tail and case with dry lint-free cloth.
2. Hard reset: place both buds in case, **keep lid open**, triple-tap **left** bud, then triple-tap **right** bud; LEDs flash red/white. Re-pair.
3. If only one side pairs: **forget device** on phone and reset above.

### Audio lag on video

Enable **gaming / low-latency mode** in TechCart app (adds slight battery use).

### Sweat after gym: crackling

Dry buds fully before case. Do **not** charge wet buds.
