# TechCart Soundbar Mini (2.1) — Setup & FAQ — India

SKU: **TC-SOUNDBAR-MINI**

## Connections

- **HDMI eARC** → preferred for TV audio + volume sync.
- **Optical (Toslink)** → if TV has no eARC; select **PCM stereo** in TV audio settings (not bitstream for voice clarity issues).
- **Bluetooth 5.3** → phone/tablet music; **not** for TV if lip-sync matters—use HDMI/optical.

## Power

Bundled adapter **19 V** centre-positive. **Do not** use random laptop chargers.

## Placement

Wall-mount or shelf; keep **≥10 cm** clearance behind subwoofer port.

## Troubleshooting

### Lip sync behind video

1. TV settings: **audio delay / AV sync** adjust +20 to +80 ms.
2. If using Bluetooth to TV adapter—switch to **wired**.

### Subwoofer hum (50 Hz ground loop)

Try different wall socket; if persists, optical instead of HDMI on that TV.

### Night volume jumps

Disable **Dolby Volume / Night mode** on TV; enable **Night mode** only inside TechCart Remote app.

## Compatibility

Works with **Fire TV Stick**, **Apple TV**, **most Android TVs**; for **JioFiber STB** use HDMI ARC port labelled on TV.
