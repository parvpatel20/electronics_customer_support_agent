# TechCart Fit V3 Smartwatch — India User Manual (summary)

SKU: **TCFIT-V3-WATCH**

## Highlights

- 1.4" AMOLED, SpO2, heart rate, sleep stages, GPS track (phone-assisted), 5 ATM swim rating **in fresh water only**.

## First setup

1. Charge to **>20%** before first boot.
2. Install **TechCart Wear (India)** from Play Store / App Store.
3. Pair watch → allow **location** (for weather and workout maps).

## Daily use

- **Crown press**: app drawer.
- **Side button**: workout quick start (customisable in app).

## India-specific tips

### SMS / call alerts delayed

Disable battery optimisation for **TechCart Wear** (Android: Settings → Apps → Battery → Unrestricted).

### Weather wrong city

Set home city manually in app → Profile → Units & region → **IST**, city search.

### Strava / Google Fit

Enable export in **TechCart Wear → Connected apps**; first sync can take **10–15 minutes**.

## Troubleshooting

### GPS track jagged indoors

GPS needs open sky; treadmill mode uses accelerometer only.

### Overnight SpO2 missing

Band must be **one finger width below wrist bone**, snug not tight; enable **night SpO2** in sleep settings.

### Watch overheating on charger

Use only bundled cable or **5 V 1–2 A** adapter; **avoid** laptop USB when closed-lid sleeping.

## Warranty

**12 months** on module defects; strap wear and water damage from **salt / hot shower** excluded.
